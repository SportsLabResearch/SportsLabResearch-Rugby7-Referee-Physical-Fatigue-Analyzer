# Metrics

The analyzer combines external-load, internal-load and mechanical-load indicators.

| Metric | Typical unit | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Match duration | min | Estimated active observation duration |
| Total distance | m | Accumulated locomotor volume |
| Metres per minute | m/min | Relative running intensity |
| High-speed distance/activity | m or relative value | Exposure to demanding locomotor actions |
| Mean heart rate | bpm | Average internal cardiovascular response |
| Maximum heart rate | bpm | Peak recorded cardiovascular response |
| Mean AcelT | a.u. | Mean instantaneous mechanical load |
| Total AcelT | a.u. | Accumulated mechanical volume |
| Relative AcelT | AcelT/min | Mechanical intensity normalised by duration |

## Why combine metrics?

A referee may maintain total volume while relative intensity falls, or show stable external load with a higher cardiovascular response. Examining several indicators reduces the risk of interpreting one noisy signal as fatigue.

## Units and comparability

Only compare files when variables and units are equivalent. Vendor exports can use different labels, sampling frequencies or definitions even when the visible metric name is similar.
